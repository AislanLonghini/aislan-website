## Platform

web

## Users

- **Executive Recruiters & C-Level / Board Decision Makers**: Evaluating senior leadership candidates for IT Director, Delivery Manager, Head of Technology, and Digital Transformation executive positions.
- **Enterprise & Financial Institutions**: Seeking high-stakes strategic consulting and advisory in Core Banking modernization, SAFe 6.0 agile scaling, and AI adoption.
- **Industry Peers & Professional Network**: Engaging for collaborative initiatives, technical-business panels, and executive networking.

## Product Purpose

- Establish an authoritative, high-impact digital presence for Aislan Longhini that clearly articulates the bridge between commercial strategy, technology delivery, and AI innovation.
- Enable evaluators to quickly navigate and validate executive credentials, regulatory achievements, and real-world corporate transformations.
- Facilitate high-value connections through direct contact channels (LinkedIn, WhatsApp, Email) and comprehensive executive profile access.

## Positioning

- **The Nexialist Executive Profile**: A rare synergy uniting 10 years of corporate commercial leadership and market expansion with 8+ years directing mission-critical banking and fintech technology initiatives (Core Banking migrations, IFRS 9 / Resolução 4966, micro front-ends, and scaled governance).
- **Verified Academic & Institutional Authority**: MIT Professional Education honors graduate in Digital Transformation and member of the global Knowledge Hub, Certified SAFe® 6.0 Practice Consultant / RTE, and Top 1 ranked Lean Agile Coach.
- **Tier-1 Social Proof**: Endorsed across all organizational levels—from MIT faculty and banking C-Level / Superintendents to frontline Tech Leads and Architects.

## Operating Context

- Reviewed by executives, board members, and senior recruiters under time constraints (30–60 second initial scan) before diving into detailed evidence.
- Accessed on both desktop workstations during structured reviews and mobile devices via shared links, messages, and QR codes.
- Must project the credibility and security mindset expected by regulated financial institutions while showcasing modern digital agility.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- **Architecture**: Single-Page React application (React 19, Tailwind CSS, Vite) structured into modular sections: Sobre, Competências, Timeline, Certificações, Recomendações e Contato.
- **Data Integrity**: All career milestones, credentials, and testimonials reflect real-world professional history; no fabricated claims or placeholder endorsements.
- **Interactivity**: Dynamic tab navigation, responsive layout, integrated contact dialog, and direct links to professional verification.

## Brand Commitments

- **Identity**: Aislan Longhini — IT Executive | Delivery Manager | Digital Transformation & Core Banking Leader.
- **Tone & Voice**: Strategic, polished, articulate, results-driven, and collaborative.
- **Incumbent Visual Identity**: Dark aesthetic (`#080B11`) with cyan, blue, and slate accents, reflecting modern technology, clarity, and institutional trust.

## Product Principles

- **Substance Over Hype**: Prioritize proven corporate results, regulatory milestones, and verifiable leadership achievements over buzzwords.
- **Scannable Precision, Deep Evidence**: Allow immediate comprehension of executive value within seconds, supported by robust evidence for thorough scrutiny.
- **Frictionless Executive Conversion**: Maintain immediate, frictionless pathways to communication (WhatsApp, LinkedIn, Email) and resume review.
- **Institutional Rigor with Modern Agility**: Reflect the governance standards of enterprise banking combined with modern product craftsmanship.
